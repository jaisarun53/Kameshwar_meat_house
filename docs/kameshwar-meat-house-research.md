## Cultural and place context for the website

`Katiya` is strongly associated with `Katahariya` in `Rautahat`. Multiple web sources describe Katahariya as the place of origin of this dish or as a place famous for it.

Common points repeated across sources:

- Katiya is a traditional meat dish cooked in a small clay pot over charcoal or a coal bed.
- The dish is associated with the Madhesh/Terai region.
- It is commonly described as a mutton dish.
- It is often served with rice, roti/chapati, chiura, or bhooja.
- Katahariya is publicly described as a place famous for `katiya`.

This gives us a strong storytelling angle for the website:

- birthplace of flavor
- clay-pot heritage
- fire, smoke, earth, charcoal, and Madhesh identity
- local tradition rather than generic restaurant branding
